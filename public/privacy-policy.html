Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Tasker AGF collects the following information when you use our service:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and authentication credentials provided via Google Sign-In or direct registration.
  • Task Data: Tasks, projects, contexts, tags, and settings you create within the app.
  • Calendar Data: If you connect Google Calendar, we access your events to enable bi-directional task synchronization.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively to provide and improve the Tasker AGF service. Your task data is stored locally on your device for offline access and synced to our secure cloud database (Firebase) to enable cross-device usage and collaboration.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is hosted on Google Cloud Platform via Firebase. We employ industry-standard security measures including strict access controls and encryption in transit and at rest.

4. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

You have the right to:

  • Access and export your personal data at any time via the Settings menu.
  • Permanently delete your account and all associated data via the Settings menu.
  • Revoke Google Calendar access directly from your Google Account security settings.
